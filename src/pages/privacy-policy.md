# Privacy Policy

**Arclink Solutions Pte. Ltd.**
Last updated: 6 August 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Arclink Solutions Pte. Ltd. ("Arclink", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, discloses and protects your personal data when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, or apply to join our operator network.

By submitting your information through our website, you confirm that you have read and understood this Policy. Where required by law, we will obtain your consent before collecting or processing your personal data.

In this Policy, "personal data" means data, whether true or not, about an individual who can be identified from that data, or from that data and other information to which we have or are likely to have access.

## 2. Who We Are

Arclink Solutions Pte. Ltd. is a company incorporated in Singapore and is the data controller responsible for your personal data.

## 3. Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal data that you provide to us directly, as well as certain data collected automatically.

**a. If you are a prospective client (submitting an enquiry):**
name, company name, work email, phone number, and the details you include in your project description (such as target locations, timeline and budget).

**b. If you apply to join our network (operator / collector / partner):**
name or contact person, email, phone number, preferred contact channel and the associated handle or number, country and city, areas you can cover, task types you can perform, availability, and — for organisations — organisation name and team size, together with any experience or resources you describe.

**c. Collected automatically when you use our website:**
IP address, browser and device information, and usage data collected through cookies and similar technologies (see Section 10).

We do not intentionally collect special categories of data (such as health, religion or biometric data). Please do not submit such information through our forms.

## 4. How and Why We Use Your Personal Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes, relying on the legal bases indicated (the legal-basis references apply where GDPR is applicable):

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Respond to your enquiry and provide requested information | Consent / Steps prior to a contract |
| Evaluate and process operator/partner applications, and contact you about opportunities | Consent / Steps prior to a contract |
| Match operators to client tasks and manage our network | Legitimate interests / Contract |
| Communicate with you via your chosen channel | Consent / Legitimate interests |
| Maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse | Legitimate interests / Legal obligation |
| Comply with applicable laws and respond to lawful requests | Legal obligation |

We will not use your personal data for purposes incompatible with those above without informing you.

## 5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

- **Service providers** who help us operate our website and process submissions — for example hosting, database, email and analytics providers — who are permitted to use the data only to provide services to us.
- **Clients**, where you are an operator and we introduce you for a specific engagement, limited to what is necessary for that engagement.
- **Professional advisers, authorities or regulators**, where required by law or to protect our legal rights.
- **Successors**, in connection with a merger, acquisition or reorganisation of our business.

## 6. International Data Transfers

Arclink operates globally and our service providers may store or process data on servers located outside your country, including outside Singapore and the European Economic Area (EEA). Where we transfer personal data internationally, we take steps to ensure an appropriate level of protection, such as relying on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses where applicable.

## 7. Data Retention

We keep your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, including to contact you about opportunities and to meet legal, accounting or reporting requirements. When your data is no longer needed, we will delete or anonymise it. If you ask us to delete your data, we will do so unless we are required to retain it by law.

## 8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS), restricted access, and storage with reputable providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; while we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law (including the Singapore PDPA and, where applicable, the GDPR), you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, where processing is based on consent;
- **Erase** your data ("right to be forgotten"), where applicable;
- **Object to** or **restrict** certain processing;
- **Data portability**, where applicable.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [dpo@arclink.com]. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Singapore, the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC); in the EEA, your local data protection authority.

## 10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate our website, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used (for example through analytics). You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect how the website functions.

## 11. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

## 12. Children

Our website and services are not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a minor has provided us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.
